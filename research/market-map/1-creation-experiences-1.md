## Synthesis Signals

**1. The consumer AI-video app thesis died in March 2026.** Sora's shutdown (peak ~1M users → <500K, ~$1M/day burn, Disney's $1B pact evaporating) is the cycle's reality check: AI video as a *feed* failed; AI video as a *tool* (Kling, Runway, CapCut, ad platforms) is where revenue actually is. Pika's drift toward consumer social now looks exposed rather than prescient.

**2. Aggregators are out-monetizing model owners at the app layer.** Freepik/Magnific ($230M ARR, zero own frontier models), Higgsfield ([EST] $200–500M annualized), Krea, and now Figma Weave all sell *access + workflow* over other people's models. Model-switching is a feature, not a bug — which pushes model makers (Runway→world models, Luma→HDR/EXR pro deliverables, Lightricks→open weights) to differentiate below or beside the app layer. Value is accruing to (a) distribution owners and (b) whoever owns the pro workflow surface; the undifferentiated middle (thin wrappers with one model) is commoditizing.

**3. Ad-creative SaaS is being crushed between platforms and authenticity.** Meta's end-2026 full-automation roadmap, Google Asset Studio/Veo, and Amazon's free Creative Studio give away performance creative. Meanwhile Icon's pivot from "AI Admaker" to "**Human** Admaker" signals synthetic-UGC fatigue at the demand side.
