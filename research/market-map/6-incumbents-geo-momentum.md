**As of August 17, 2026.** Research slice: incumbent positions (Jan–Aug 2026 moves), geography, and a momentum list.

## PART A — Incumbent positions (Jan–Aug 2026)

### Google — the distribution juggernaut, now consolidated
**2026 moves:**
- **Gemini app passed 1B MAU** — fastest-growing product in Google history; DAU tripled YoY; Gemini generates **150M+ images/day** (Google blog, 2026: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/one-billion-monthly-users/; Search Engine Journal: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-gemini-passes-1b-monthly-users-63-now-use-voice/585624/). Veo 3 had passed 70M videos generated by July 2025 (pre-2026 baseline; Business of Apps: https://www.businessofapps.com/data/google-gemini-statistics/).
- **Flow relaunched as unified creative workspace (Feb 25, 2026):** Google merged Flow + Whisk + ImageFX into one idea→image→audio-synced-video pipeline; available on AI Pro/Ultra in 140+ countries (SolidAITech guide, May 2026: https://www.solidaitech.com/2026/05/google-flow.html; vidau.ai: https://www.vidau.ai/google-flow-ai-update-2026-complete-guide/).
- **Project Genie launched Jan 28, 2026 (US):** consumer access to Genie 3 real-time interactive world model, gated behind the **$249.99/mo AI Ultra** tier (9to5Google, Jan 29, 2026: https://9to5google.com/2026/01/29/google-project-genie/; DeepMind: https://deepmind.google/models/genie/). Early criticism it produces "playable Nintendo knockoffs" (TechBuzz: https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/google-s-project-genie-creates-playable-nintendo-knockoffs) — IP exposure in world models is an open question.
**Where advantages bind:** distribution (Gemini app, YouTube, Android, Workspace), inference cost (TPUs), and bundling video gen into a $20–$250/mo subscription ladder. Veo/Flow is now the default "serious" consumer/prosumer video tool in most of the world — and Sora's exit (below) handed Google the western consumer field largely uncontested.
**Where stalled:** no breakout *social* format of its own (YouTube Shorts integration is additive, not category-defining); Genie is expensive-tier and US-only.

### OpenAI — the biggest incumbent failure of 2026: Sora shut down
- **Sora app discontinued.** OpenAI said goodbye to the Sora app on **March 24, 2026**; web/app closed **April 26, 2026**; **Sora API sunsets September 24, 2026** — the end of the Sora brand (The Decoder: https://the-decoder.com/openai-sets-two-stage-sora-shutdown-with-app-closing-april-2026-and-api-following-in-september/; OpenAI Help Center: https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation).
- **Why it failed:** ~**$1M/day operating cost vs ~$2.1M total revenue** [reported by press, not OpenAI]; DAU peaked ~1M then fell below 500K; downloads −70% from November; novelty decay plus copyright/deepfake blowback (Kotaku: https://kotaku.com/openais-video-plagiarism-app-sora-was-reportedly-losing-1-million-a-day-2000683277; CNN, Mar 26, 2026: https://edition.cnn.com/2026/03/26/business/openai-sora-consumer-misread). Monetization arrived late ($4/10 extra generations; TechBuzz: https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/openai-admits-sora-economics-are-unsustainable-launches-paid-credits). Android launch (470K first-day downloads; Engadget: https://www.engadget.com/ai/openais-sora-app-is-now-available-on-android-191722821.html) couldn't reverse retention decay.
- **Conflict flag:** Disney signed a deal allowing Sora to generate Disney-character videos on **Dec 11, 2025** (TechCrunch: https://techcrunch.com/?s=disney+midjourney search result), yet shutdown coverage cites "a collapsed Disney deal" as a contributing factor (techflowpost: https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/30920) — sequence appears to be: deal signed, then unraveled in early 2026. Verify before quoting.
- **What remains:** image generation is still strategic — **"ChatGPT Images 2.0"** shipped after a Dec 16, 2025 "code red" image-model push; it is notably strong at in-image text and a hit in India, muted elsewhere (TechCrunch, Apr 21 & Apr 30, 2026: https://techcrunch.com/?s=gpt-image). OpenAI's pivot narrative: away from consumer creative tools, toward coding/enterprise ahead of a possible IPO (MindStudio: https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/openai-shutting-down-sora-what-happened).
**Lesson investors are drawing:** raw model quality + app-store spike ≠ durable consumer media franchise; unit economics and retention killed the category leader in six months.

### Meta — from licensing to in-house ("Muse"), aimed at advertisers
- **Vibes** (AI video feed, launched Sept 25, 2025) initially ran on **licensed Midjourney and Black Forest Labs models** (Magic Hour: https://magichour.ai/blog/meta-ais-vibes-feed; SocialMediaToday: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-partners-with-midjourney-ai-visual-tools/758551/).
- **July 7, 2026: Meta launched its own image model "Muse"** to court advertisers and subscribers, explicitly saying it will **reduce reliance on Midjourney/BFL licensing** (CNBC, Jul 7, 2026: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/07/meta-ai-muse-image.html). Midjourney's licensing revenue from Meta is therefore likely transitional.
- **Ad machine:** Meta now **requires AI-generated-creative disclosure** on ads in 2026 (OneDot ABM: https://dhruboduti.com/blog/ai-generated-ad-creative-now-requires-disclosure-on-meta-what-counts-and-how-to-comply); agencies report AI-industrialized creative shipping 4x faster with 30–70% CTR lifts [est., agency-reported] (Market IA: https://www.market-ia.fr/en/blog/meta-ads-ai-creatives-guide-2026).
**Where advantages bind:** ad system + performance data — the "infinite creative" pipeline where the model is optimized against auction outcomes, not aesthetics. That data moat is unavailable to any startup.
**Where stalled:** Movie Gen never shipped as a standalone product (no 2026 evidence of a Movie Gen release found); Vibes has cultural-cringe problems and no evidence of breakout retention; Meta was a model-licensing *customer*, not a leader, until mid-2026.

### Adobe — the quiet monetization winner
- **AI-First ARR >$500M, up >3x YoY**; Firefly ending ARR approaching **$300M** (+~50% QoQ via Firefly apps/credit packs); Firefly enterprise-generated assets **+4x YoY** (Coca-Cola, SAP, Tesco custom models); **GenStudio ending ARR +25% YoY**; FY26 revenue target raised to $20.5–20.6B (Adobe Q2 FY26 call, Jun 11, 2026: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/06/11/adobe-adbe-q2-2026-earnings-transcript/; MarketScale: https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/adobes-firefly-driven-ai-first-arr-has-tripled-signaling-a-shift-from-disruption-target-to-monetization-platform).
- **Partner-model aggregation is the strategy:** Firefly/Express/CC integrate models from **Google, OpenAI, Black Forest Labs, Luma, Runway, Topaz, ElevenLabs** — Adobe as the neutral front-end for everyone's models (same earnings sources). Adobe Ventures also joined Runway's Series E (Crunchbase News: https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/gen-ai-video-startup-unicorn-runway-seriese-raise/).
**Where advantages bind:** enterprise workflow lock-in, brand-safety/indemnification, and being the aggregation layer (selling everyone's models inside its UI). **Where stalled:** Adobe's own frontier-model ambitions — its first-party models are no longer the pitch; the pitch is the workflow.

### ByteDance — global-scale shipping machine
- **Seedance 2.0 shipped inside CapCut** (paid tiers) across Brazil, SE Asia, MENA, LatAm, Africa in **March 2026** (TechCrunch, Mar 26, 2026: https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/26/bytedances-new-ai-video-generation-model-dreamina-seedance-2-0-comes-to-capcut/).
- **Seedance 2.5 launched globally on Dreamina, July 31, 2026:** up to **30s continuous generation, 50 multimodal reference assets**, in-workflow targeted editing (Yahoo Finance/PR: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/dreamina-launches-seedance-2-5-131400657.html). Naming: Jimeng (CN) = Dreamina (intl); Seedance = video model, Seedream = image model (howaiworks: https://howaiworks.ai/blog/jimeng-dreamina-seedance-bytedance-ai-video).
**Where advantages bind:** CapCut/TikTok distribution to billions, plus the only true consumer-creation funnel (create → publish → monetize in one company). Notably shipping AI video to *emerging markets first*.
**Where constrained:** US divest-or-ban politics keeps TikTok/CapCut's US AI rollout cautious; Seedance 2.0 skipped the US/EU initially.

### Canva — design mass-market, now with pro tools free
- **$42B valuation** via Aug 2025 employee secondary (up from $37B, Jul 2025) (TSG Invest: https://tsginvest.com/canva/).
- **Affinity made completely free** (photo/vector/layout studio) — a direct shot at Adobe's prosumer base (posterly guide: https://www.poster.ly/guides/canva-guide; felloai: https://felloai.com/canva-pricing/).
- Leonardo.AI acquisition (Jul 2024) supplies the Phoenix model powering Magic Studio image/video; enterprise tier (50+ seats, 400 premium AI uses/person); claimed **95% Fortune 500 penetration** [company-claimed reach, not paid enterprise contracts — treat carefully] (posterly, above).
**Where advantages bind:** 200M+ mass-market users who don't care which model renders the image; AI as feature, not product. **Where stalled:** no frontier model of its own; video generation is licensed/derivative.

### NVIDIA — arms dealer + kingmaker
- **Cosmos 3 launched June 1, 2026:** open "omnimodel" world foundation model (text/image/video/ambient audio/action) for physical AI + synthetic data (NVIDIA IR, Jun 1, 2026: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-cosmos-3-the-open-frontier-foundation-model-for-physical-ai). **Cosmos Coalition** includes Black Forest Labs, Runway, LTX (Lightricks) — GenMedia startups joining NVIDIA's world-model orbit.
- Investment footprint across the map: Runway Series E, Synthesia Series E, BFL Series B (see those entries). NVIDIA is effectively index-long the GenMedia model layer.
**Where advantages bind:** every video model trains and serves on NVIDIA; world-model convergence (video gen ↔ robotics sim) plays to its platform. **Where it doesn't:** no consumer surface, and Google's TPU-served Veo is the biggest inference pool it doesn't touch.

### Microsoft — late but now shipping in-house
- **Build 2026: seven in-house MAI models** (reasoning, code, image, transcribe, voice) in Microsoft Foundry; **MAI-Image-2.5** claims Arena score above Nano Banana Pro [company-claimed] (Microsoft AI blog: https://microsoft.ai/news/building-a-hillclimbing-machine-launching-seven-new-mai-models/; MindStudio explainer: https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/microsoft-mai-models-explained-build-2026). No MAI *video* model found as of Aug 2026; Muse (game world model, 2025) has no major 2026 consumer follow-through found.
**Where advantages bind:** Azure/Foundry enterprise catalog + M365/Copilot seat distribution. **Where stalled:** consumer GenMedia — Microsoft has no creative consumer franchise, and analysts called the MAI portfolio "credible, not category-defining" (Windows Forum: https://windowsforum.com/threads/microsoft-build-2026-mai-models-credible-ai-portfolio-not-yet-category-defining.423536/).

### Amazon — commerce-creative, not frontier
- Nova Canvas/Reel remain Bedrock's in-house creative models (6s clips, camera controls; expanded to Europe Mar 2025); primary strategic use is **Amazon Ads**: letting sellers auto-generate display/video creative, "keyword-level creative" (AWS Industries blog: https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/industries/supercharging-ad-creative-with-amazon-bedrock-and-amazon-nova-how-ai-is-revolutionizing-content-generation-for-advertising-marketing-use-cases; About Amazon: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/amazon-nova-artificial-intelligence-bedrock-aws). No evidence found of a 2026 frontier-quality Nova video upgrade competitive with Veo/Seedance.
**Where advantages bind:** retail-ads flywheel (millions of sellers who need "good enough" creative) + Bedrock as neutral model catalog. **Where stalled:** model quality; nobody picks Nova Reel on merit.

### Apple — finally credible, privately-clouded
- **WWDC 2026 (Jun 8):** Image Playground overhauled with **photorealistic generation** via a new model on **Private Cloud Compute**, touch-to-edit, and **SynthID watermarking** (notable: Google's watermark standard inside Apple's stack); ships free with iOS 27 this fall (AppleInsider, Jun 8, 2026: https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/06/08/image-playground-gets-realistic-ai-image-generation-in-ios-27; PetaPixel: https://petapixel.com/2026/06/08/apples-all-new-image-playground-promises-more-than-cartoons/).
**Where advantages bind:** default distribution on ~1.5B devices + privacy posture. **Where stalled:** two years behind on quality; no video generation; no creator/pro story.

## Synthesis signals

**Where incumbent advantages actually bind:**
1. **Distribution + bundling (Google):** video gen as a feature of a 1B-MAU assistant and a $20–250/mo subscription ladder beats any standalone app on CAC. Sora's death proves the standalone consumer AI-video app is nearly unbuildable at frontier cost structures — even for OpenAI.
2. **Ad-system data (Meta, Amazon, Snap):** generative creative optimized against auction outcomes is a closed loop startups can't enter. This is where "infinite creative" value accrues silently.
3. **Enterprise workflow + indemnification (Adobe, Canva, Microsoft):** Adobe's >$500M AI-first ARR shows the money is in governed workflow, and Adobe aggregates *startups'* models — the model layer is being commoditized upward into UI owners.
4. **Compute economics:** Google (TPUs) and ByteDance (scale) can run video inference at costs that killed Sora ($1M/day). Cost-per-generation is the real moat variable in video.

**Where startups can still build $10B+ companies:**
1. **Audio** — ElevenLabs ($500M ARR, $11B) proves a full-stack modality winner can exist where incumbents under-invested; music (Suno $5.4B) is following despite legal overhang.
2. **Revenue-first video platforms with owned distribution economics** — Kling monetizes through Kuaishou but sells globally; PixVerse/Hailuo show consumer video works when inference cost and price are matched (a discipline Chinese players have and Sora didn't).
3. **Enterprise vertical video** — Synthesia/HeyGen: boring, governed, 140% NRR businesses beneath the incumbents' attention.
4. **Infra/inference layer** — fal.ai at $4B+: every model war increases its TAM; model-agnostic.
5. **Open-weights + world models** — BFL and LTX monetize being the neutral open standard (incl. via NVIDIA's Cosmos orbit) as video and simulation converge.

**Geography power balance:** The US owns platforms/distribution and enterprise monetization; **China now owns consumer AI-video revenue and export apps** (Kling, Hailuo, PixVerse, Dreamina — plus the first IPOs); **Europe owns two durable verticals** (audio via ElevenLabs, open image via BFL, enterprise video via Synthesia) but no consumer platform; Israel punches above weight in open video (LTX). Regulation is converging on mandatory provenance (China Sept 2025, EU Aug 2026) — a tailwind for watermarking infra and for incumbents with compliance machinery, a tax on small consumer apps serving the EU.

**Single biggest structural fact of 2026:** the market bifurcated — *west monetizes GenMedia as enterprise software and platform features; China monetizes it as direct consumer/creator revenue.* The Sora shutdown (Apr 2026) and Kling's $500M ARR (Mar 2026), five weeks apart, are the two data points that define the map.
