## 1. Gaming & Interactive Media

### Category shape (2026)
Two distinct sub-markets have emerged: (a) **production tooling** for studios (assets, NPCs, dialogue) — modest, steady; and (b) **generative/playable worlds** — the hot capital magnet. The world-model category alone reportedly attracted **>$3B of investment in 2026** across Decart, Odyssey, World Labs, and Yann LeCun's AMI Labs ([TheNextWeb, May 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/decart-300-million-radical-ventures-world-models)). One tracker pegs generative-AI-in-gaming at ~$2.21B in 2026 vs $1.79B in 2025 (estimate, [cognitivefuture.ai](https://cognitivefuture.ai/best-ai-tools-for-game-development/)).

### Playable / generative worlds
- **Google DeepMind — Genie 3 / Project Genie** — *category leader (research → product)*. Genie 3 (announced Aug 2025) generates navigable photorealistic worlds from text at 720p/24fps with minutes-long consistency. **"Project Genie" shipped as a consumer-facing playable product to US AI Ultra subscribers on Jan 29, 2026** ([9to5Google, 2026-01-29](https://9to5google.com/2026/01/29/google-project-genie/); [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/project-genie/)). Business model: bundled into the premium Ultra subscription — worlds as a retention feature, not standalone revenue. Own models (obviously). Moat: frontier world-model research + TPU compute + distribution.
- **Decart** — Israeli lab behind Oasis (real-time playable Minecraft-like world, Oct 2024). 2026 pivot up-market: **raised $300M at ~$4B valuation, May 2026** (Radical Ventures lead; Nvidia, Adobe Ventures, Toyota Ventures, Sequoia, Benchmark; >$450M total) ([SiliconANGLE, 2026-05-18](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/18/decart-raises-300m-ai-optimization-software-world-models/)). Launched **Oasis 3** (June 2026): photorealistic *driving-simulation* world model via API — i.e., pivoting from consumer toy to AV/simulation infrastructure ([TechCrunch, 2026-06-10](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/10/decarts-new-world-model-can-simulate-hours-of-photorealistic-driving-with-some-caveats/)). Also offers "Lucy" for immersive/consumer experiences. **Aug 2026: reported Anthropic acquisition talks at ~$6B** ([TechTimes, 2026-08-13](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324234/20260813/anthropic-talks-acquire-decart-6b-targeting-77-gross-margins-before-ipo.htm)) — single-source, unconfirmed; treat as rumor.
- **Odyssey** — interactive-video world model startup; **raised $310M at $1.45B valuation, June 2026**, repositioning toward "AI model simulation" ([SiliconANGLE, 2026-06-17](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/17/odyssey-raises-310m-1-45b-valuation-transform-ai-model-simulation/)). Revenue: not publicly reported. Note the pattern: like Decart, monetization is drifting from entertainment toward simulation/training-data infrastructure.
- **Microsoft — Muse** — WHAM gameplay model (Feb 2025, Nature paper; trained on Bleeding Edge) ([Xbox Wire, 2025-02-19](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/02/19/muse-ai-xbox-empowering-creators-and-players/)). No evidence found of a shipping consumer product through Aug 2026 — positioned as developer/preservation research; Xbox messaging is "help developers, not replace them" ([eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/microsoft-muse-generative-ai-model-gameplay/)). Community reception remains skeptical ([Gamefile](https://www.gamefile.news/p/microsoft-xbox-muse-generative-ai)).
- **Runway — Game Worlds** — the notable cross-category pivot: video-model leader Runway launched **Game Worlds** (beta), real-time text+image interactive narrative games, with stated plans to add generated video/gameplay ([Runway Research](https://runwayml.com/research/runway-game-worlds)). Early/beta; revenue impact not publicly reported.

### Studio tooling (assets, NPCs)
- **Scenario** — custom fine-tuned asset generation for studios (style-locked models from ~15 reference images; $15–$100/mo tiers). Still the reference for style-consistent 2D assets in 2026 roundups ([TECHSY 2026](https://techsy.io/en/blog/best-ai-game-asset-generators)). Scale: not publicly reported; no 2026 funding found. Model dependency: fine-tunes on diffusion backbones (multi-model). Moat: workflow + private style models — thin vs. frontier image models.
- **Layer AI** — team-oriented game-art pipeline (style adherence, tileable textures, engine export); consistently ranked alongside Scenario ([Sonilo 2026](https://sonilo.com/blog/comparisons/best-ai-game-asset-generators)). Scale: not publicly reported.
- **Inworld AI** — NPC/character infrastructure leader → broadened into real-time voice agents. **~$120M cumulative funding, $500M valuation** (Lightspeed, Samsung Next, LG Tech Ventures et al.) ([Tracxn 2026](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/inworld-ai/__tjWZC3FjI2Vxjo9ijvchq0joN7t3zWc5nHkAN9FWwuk)); a 2026 newsletter headline claims "record funding for AI x gaming" ([Nifty Sparks substack](https://niftysparks.substack.com/p/inworld-makes-history-with-record-funding-for-ai-gaming-nifty-sparks-news)) — round details unverified in my searches; flag for confirmation. Described in 2026 guides as "the leading infrastructure layer for AI-driven characters." Revenue: not publicly reported. Business model: B2B API/licensing. Notably diversifying *out* of games into voice agents — a tell about game-studio budget depth.
- **Convai** — conversational NPCs with perception/actions; active, often paired with **NVIDIA ACE**, which now ships on-device in titles like PUBG and Total War: Pharaoh ([Librancy 2026](https://librancy.ai/best-ai-tools/gaming-3d)). Scale: not publicly reported.

### AI-native games & narrative platforms
- **Rosebud AI** — evolved into a **text-to-game "vibe coding" engine** (describe a game → code/logic/physics generated). Funding: **$9.2M total** (PitchBook) — tiny relative to mindshare ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/343168-12); [Converge review 2026](https://combos.converge.ai/blog/rosebud-ai-game-creator-review)). Third-party LLM dependent. Moat: community/templates; weak defensibility vs Cursor-style general tools.
- **Latitude / AI Dungeon** — alive and actively shipping in 2026; added models incl. "GLM 5.1" and image gen; community scenario library ([dungeonsdeep.ai review, 2026](https://dungeonsdeep.ai/blog/ai-dungeon-review-2026)). Subscription consumer model; scale not publicly reported. Survivor, not breakout.
- **Hidden Door** — AI social roleplaying in **licensed fictional worlds** (tabletop-RPG-style, graphic-novel panels); launched publicly 2025 ([Variety, 2025](https://variety.com/2025/gaming/news/hidden-door-ai-role-playing-fan-fiction-game-platform-1236488265/); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/hidden-door-ai-game-narrative-rpg/)). Differentiator: IP licensing as moat — the licensed-content thesis mirrors what happened in music. Scale: not publicly reported.
- **Websim** — prompt-to-web-experience sandbox ("hallucinated internet"), multi-model (Claude/GPT). Still active in 2026 tool listings but no funding/scale news found; cultural moment appears to have passed to general vibe-coding tools ([Coda One 2026](https://www.codaone.ai/tools/websim/)). "Vibe-coded games" became a real scene: Cursor's Vibe Jam 2026 required ≥90% AI-generated code ([vibejam.com](https://vibejam.com/)); new entrant **Nilo** (browser 3D creation) opened beta Apr 2026 ([nilo.io](https://nilo.io/articles/play-vibe-coded-games-2026)).
- **Series AI** — no substantive 2026 news surfaced in searches; status unverified (was building Rho Engine with major-publisher deals as of 2024/25). Flag: needs direct check.

**Category read**: tooling is commoditizing (Scenario/Layer/Rosebud all under-capitalized vs the value they'd need to defend); NPC infra is consolidating around NVIDIA ACE + Inworld; the capital and the story moved to world models, which are themselves pivoting to *simulation infrastructure* (AV, robotics) because consumer playable-worlds monetization is unproven outside Google's subscription bundle.

## 2. Social / UGC / Consumer Entertainment

### The defining event: Sora app shutdown
- **OpenAI Sora app** — launched Sept 30, 2025 as an invite-only AI-video social feed; fastest-to-1M-downloads ever (beating ChatGPT); #1 App Store. Then collapsed: downloads peaked ~3.3M/mo (Nov 2025), fell ~45% by Jan 2026, ~1.1M by Feb. **OpenAI announced shutdown March 24, 2026** citing declining engagement + unresolved deepfake/moderation problems ([Axios, 2026-03-24](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/24/openai-discontinue-sora-video-app); [TechCrunch, 2026-03-24](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/openais-sora-was-the-creepiest-app-on-your-phone-now-its-shutting-down/)). Reported in-app purchase revenue over its life: ~$2.1M (estimate). One report claims a Disney licensing deal (200+ characters, $1B investment) was scuttled by the shutdown ([bitcoinworld/cryptorank](https://bitcoinworld.co.in/openai-sora-app-shutdown-2026/)) — single-source, treat cautiously. Sora as a *model/API* continues (e.g., HeyGen B-roll integration); it's the *social app* that died.
- **Meta — Vibes** (AI-only video feed in Meta AI app, launched Sept 25, 2025). Reached ~2M DAU by Nov 2025, growth concentrated in emerging markets; engagement data suggests novelty-driven usage and weak retention; relentlessly criticized as "AI slop," but Meta kept promoting AI content algorithmically ([WinBuzzer, 2025-11-18](https://winbuzzer.com/2025/11/18/metas-vibes-ai-feed-hits-2-million-daily-users-fueled-by-ai-slop-strategy-in-emerging-markets-xcxwbn/); [SocialMediaToday](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/data-shows-that-metas-ai-based-vibes-feed-is-struggling-to-hold-attentio/805862/)). Still live as of mid-2026; no shutdown found. Business model: ad-fed attention; moat: distribution only.
- **xAI — Grok Imagine** — the surprise consumer winner among model-owned apps. **#1 on Artificial Analysis Video Arena (text-to-video and image-to-video) by late Jan 2026**; Video 1.5 (summer 2026) retook #1 image-to-video ([Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Grok_Imagine); [the-ai-corner](https://www.the-ai-corner.com/p/grok-imagine-number-one-ai-video-model-guide)). Aggressive pricing: ~$4.20/min of video vs Sora 2 Pro $30 and Veo 3.1 $12 (reported comparison, [invideo.io](https://invideo.io/blog/grok-imagine/)). Distribution through X; June 2026 moved to a unified usage pool across Grok products. Own models. Usage/revenue: not publicly reported.
- **Higgsfield** — the standout independent consumer/prosumer breakout. Launched Mar 2025; **$1.3B valuation (Jan 2026, $80M Accel-led extension)**; reported **$500M annualized revenue run-rate by June 2026** (up from $400M in May), targeting $1B ARR exit-2026, and in talks for $300–500M at ~$5B pre ([Yahoo/Reuters, Jan 2026](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-video-startup-higgsfield-hits-120257371.html); [TechTimes, 2026-06-30](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319394/20260630/ai-video-startup-higgsfield-hits-500m-revenue-eyes-5b-funding-round.htm)). **Conflict flag**: GetLatka lists only $200M ARR for 2026 — the $500M figure is company-claimed "annualized" (spiky months); treat the run-rate as soft. Model dependency: multi-model orchestration + own effects layer; primary customer: creators/marketers; moat: speed of trend capture (camera-control effects, meme formats), which is real but fragile.
- **Mirage (fka Captions)** — renamed Sept 2025 to signal foundation-model ambitions for short-form video ("pacing, framing, attention" models). **$75M growth financing from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, Mar 24, 2026; $175M total raised; 20M+ users claimed** ([TechCrunch, 2026-03-24](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/24/mirage-raises-75m-to-continue-building-models-for-its-ai-video-editing-app-captions/)). Note: CVF financing is revenue-linked, implying real recurring revenue (amount not publicly reported). Own models for talking-video/editing; expanding to ads/marketing.
- **PixVerse (AIsphere)** — leading Chinese consumer AI-video app gone global. **Series C ~$300M (Mar 2026, Alibaba-backed) + extension to $439M total (July 13, 2026), valuation >$2B**; claims **150M+ registered users, 15M MAU across 177 countries**; launched **R1 real-time video generation model Jan 2026**; V5.6 ranked #2 globally on i2v/t2v ([Bloomberg, 2026-03-12](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-12/alibaba-backed-video-ai-startup-pixverse-raises-300-million); [TFN, July 2026](https://techfundingnews.com/video-generation-startup-pixverse-lands-439m-from-alibaba-and-others-to-reshape-entertainment/); [CNBC, 2026-01-13](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/13/alibaba-backed-pixverse-real-time-ai-video-generation-tool-investors-startup-openai-sora.html)). Own models. User figures are company-claimed.
- **Viggle** — character-animation/meme engine (JST-1 video-3D model with physics understanding); $19M Series A (a16z, Aug 2024) + Alphabet/AI Futures Fund on cap table; 4M+ Discord community; still widely used for short-form character swaps in 2026, but no 2026 round found ([Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/viggle-ai); [tooldirectory 2026](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/viggle-ai)). Revenue: not publicly reported.
- **Character.AI** — pivoted hard into media: **AvatarFX** video generation for all users + an AI-native social feed (launched mid-2025). Estimates: ~$32M revenue 2025 trending to ~$60M 2026; ~20M MAU, ~165M monthly visits (tracker estimates: [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/character-ai/), [demandsage](https://www.demandsage.com/character-ai-statistics/)). Under the Google licensing umbrella since Aug 2024. The chat→feed→video arc makes it a genuine GenMedia consumer platform now.
- **Platform incumbents**: **YouTube** put **Veo 3 into Shorts free** (announced Cannes Lions by CEO Neal Mohan; rollout from summer 2025 onward) — the single biggest distribution event for video gen ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/artificial-intelligence/youtube-shorts-is-getting-a-huge-free-veo-3-upgrade-that-might-just-make-me-leave-tiktok-and-capcuts-behind)). **CapCut (ByteDance)** embedded Veo 3-class generation plus its own AI stack (script-to-video, templates, TTS) into the default mobile editing workflow ([capcut.com](https://www.capcut.com/tools/veo-3-0)). Effect: free platform-bundled generation compresses the standalone consumer-app opportunity to speed/effects/community (Higgsfield) or verticalized workflow (Mirage).

**Category read**: 2026's brutal lesson — **AI-generation-as-social-network failed at the flagship level (Sora dead in 6 months; Vibes limping)**, while AI-generation-as-*tool* embedded in existing distribution (YouTube/CapCut/X) and prosumer subscription apps (Higgsfield, Mirage, PixVerse) is where usage and revenue actually accrued.

## 3. Music & Audio

### The 2026 story: litigation → licensing regime
- **Udio + UMG/WMG/Merlin** — UMG settled with Udio and announced strategic agreements (**Oct 2025**) ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/universal-music-group-and-udio-announce-udios-first-strategic-agreements-for-new-licensed-ai-music-creation-platform-302599129.html)); WMG followed with its own Udio agreement; Merlin (indie labels) signed too ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/udio-merlin-ai-music-licensing-deal/)). Udio will **retire its unlicensed model during 2026 and relaunch as a licensed, walled-garden remix/customization service** — no downloads off-platform, pivot away from net-new song generation ([Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-features/ai-music-universal-music-group-settlement-udio-1235457945/); [Udio help center](https://help.udio.com/en/articles/12683565-changes-associated-with-the-universal-music-group-umg-partnership)). Effectively a capture of Udio by the labels; independent product ambition largely ended.
- **Suno** — category leader, and the one that kept leverage. **$300M ARR and 2M paid subs (Feb 2026)**, up from $200M ARR/1M subs at the Nov 2025 Series C ($250M at $2.45B post); **100M+ total users**; then **$400M raise at $5.4B valuation (June 2026)** ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/suno/); [TechTimes, 2026-06-06](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317917/20260606/suno-raises-400-million-54-billion-valuation-while-record-labels-sue-over-61000-songs.htm)). **WMG settled its lawsuit and struck a licensing partnership** — Suno to deprecate models trained on unlicensed music, paid-only downloads, free-tier non-commercial; Suno **acquired Songkick from WMG** (Nov 2025) for live-music discovery ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/warner-music-group-settles-with-suno-strikes-first-of-its-kind-deal-with-ai-song-generator/); [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-features/suno-warner-music-group-ai-music-settlement-lawsuit-1235472868/)). **UMG also settled per mid-2026 reporting; Sony holds out** — fair-use summary-judgment hearing in D. Mass. expected ~July 2026, with labels alleging 61,000+ additional infringed songs ([TechTimes, 2026-06-09](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318087/20260609/musicians-sue-warner-universal-over-ai-settlement-money-suno-ruling-nears.htm) — note this piece also covers artists suing WMG/UMG over settlement money distribution). Earlier acquisition: WavTool DAW (June 2025) → Suno Studio. Own models; consumer subscription + creator tools; moat shifting from model quality to *licensed catalog + community + distribution*.
- **ElevenLabs** — audio's platform company. **$500M Series D at $11B valuation (Feb 4, 2026, Sequoia lead; ~$781M total raised)**; eyeing IPO ([TechCrunch, 2026-02-04](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/04/elevenlabs-raises-500m-from-sequioia-at-a-11-billion-valuation/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/04/nvidia-backed-ai-startup-elevenlabs-11-billion-valuation.html)). ARR ~$500M per GetLatka (estimate). **Eleven Music** (launched Aug 2025) went licensed-first: deals with **Merlin, Kobalt (publishing), Believe (training data)**; claims ~14M songs generated, 4,000+ artists on the ElevenMusic app, **$11M distributed to creators**; Music v2 (May 2026) cut API pricing up to 50% ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/elevenlabs-creator-of-suno-rival-eleven-music-raises-500m-at-11bn-valuation/); [Digital Music News, 2026-02-05](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/02/05/elevenlabs-series-d/)). Own models; B2B API + prosumer; moat: voice → dubbing → agents → music platform breadth + enterprise trust.
- **Adobe** — **Generate Soundtrack** (licensed instrumental music synced to video) and **Generate Speech** shipped at MAX (Oct 2025) inside Firefly's new audio/video studio, with ElevenLabs as a partner model ([MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/adobes-new-firefly-can-create-custom-fully-licensed-ai-soundtracks-for-video/); [Adobe MAX](https://news.adobe.com/news/2025/10/adobe-max-2025-firefly)). "Commercially safe" is the wedge for enterprise.
- **Voice infrastructure tier** (feeds media production even though much revenue is agents): **Cartesia** — $64M Series A (Kleiner Perkins; $91M total), Sonic 2.0 at 40–90ms latency, 10k+ customers; trackers report a later ~$200M round/IPO chatter (unconfirmed — [TechStackIPO](https://www.techstackipo.com/ipo/cartesia-ai), flag). **Rime** — $24M Series A led by M13 with Twilio Ventures (July 15, 2026) for enterprise call voices ([TechCrunch, 2026-07-15](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/rime-picks-up-24m-series-a-to-help-enterprises-field-customer-calls/)). **Resemble AI** — cloning + deepfake detection niche. **Descript** — Underlord agentic co-editor is now the product center (model-picker incl. Claude Sonnet 4.5 — i.e., third-party-LLM dependent for the agent, own audio tech underneath); no 2026 funding found ([Descript blog](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/descript-season-6-meet-underlord)).
- **Udio-adjacent/licensing-safe long tail** (Kits AI, Voice-Swap, LALAL, iZotope): no material 2026 news surfaced in searches; sub-scale relative to the above. Not publicly reported metrics.

**Category read**: The unlicensed-training era ended commercially in music in a 9-month window (Oct 2025–mid-2026). Labels converted lawsuits into equity-adjacent partnerships; the strategic split is **Suno (settled from strength, kept product control)** vs **Udio (settled into a walled garden)** vs **ElevenLabs/Adobe (licensed-first from day one)**. Sony v. Suno's fair-use ruling (summer 2026) is the remaining legal overhang.

## 4. 3D / Spatial / World Creation

- **World Labs (Fei-Fei Li)** — *category-defining*. **Marble** — multimodal world model producing persistent, navigable 3D environments *with exportable triangle meshes + collider meshes* (i.e., outputs that drop into standard 3D/physics pipelines) — limited beta Nov 2025 → **general availability Feb 2026** alongside a **$1B growth round (~$1.23B total; backers incl. NVIDIA, AMD, Fidelity, Emerson Collective; Autodesk committed $200M** and takes an advisory role) ([TechCrunch, 2025-11-12](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/12/fei-fei-lis-world-labs-speeds-up-the-world-model-race-with-marble-its-first-commercial-product/); [TheAIInsider, 2026-02-19](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/02/19/fei-fei-lis-world-labs-raises-1b-in-fresh-funding-to-advance-development-of-world-models/)). Freemium + paid tiers — notable for open access in a gated category. Revenue: not publicly reported. Moat: research team + mesh-native outputs + Autodesk channel.
- **Meshy** — 3D-asset generation leader by revenue discipline. **Series B of ~$400M at $1.5B valuation (July 21, 2026)** — largest round ever for a dedicated AI-3D company; **10M+ registered users, 100M+ models generated, ~$30M ARR** reported ([TFN](https://techfundingnews.com/from-mit-research-to-1-5b-unicorn-ethan-hus-meshy-raises-400m-for-ai-powered-3d-creation/); [3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/329350/meshy-closes-series-b-worth-nearly-400m/)). Note the ratio: $1.5B valuation on ~$30M ARR = the category is being priced on world-model optionality, not current revenue. Meshy-6 preferred in professional side-by-sides (per company/press comparisons — marketing-adjacent claim).
- **Tripo (VAST)** — closest challenger: **~$200M Series A (June 2026)**, plus an earlier $50M tranche (Mar 2026) with new production-ready models; claims 6.5M creators, 90k developers, ~100M assets ([PR Newswire, Mar 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/tripo-ai-announces-50-million-in-funding-and-new-models-for-production-ready-3d-generation-302724894.html); [TFN Meshy piece for the $200M figure](https://techfundingnews.com/from-mit-research-to-1-5b-unicorn-ethan-hus-meshy-raises-400m-for-ai-powered-3d-creation/)). Chinese-founded, global product.
- **Hyper3D / Rodin (Deemos)** — **Rodin Gen-2.5 (May 26, 2026)**: sculpt-level detail, production topology controls; flagship enterprise proof: **Lowe's 30,000+ item catalog converted 2D→3D at <$1/model** (NVIDIA case study) ([Barchart/PressAdvantage, 2026-05-26](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/2134263/hyper3d-launches-rodin-gen-2-5-bringing-sculpt-level-detail-and-production-control-to-ai-3d-generation)). E-commerce digitization is emerging as the real B2B revenue line for 3D gen. Funding: not publicly reported at scale.
- **Luma AI** — graduated from 3D capture into a full video/media lab: **$900M Series C at $4B (Nov 2025, led by Saudi Arabia's Humain — compute-for-equity dynamics; >$1B total)**; Ray3 first native 16-bit HDR video model; Ray3.14 (Jan 2026) 1080p at 4x speed; **Luma Agents (Mar 2026)** for end-to-end multi-modal projects ([startupintros](https://startupintros.com/orgs/luma-ai); [Fastio review 2026](https://fast.io/resources/luma-ai-review-2026/)). Its original 3D/NeRF product is now a side business — effectively *left* this category for video.
- **Spline** ($10M Series A, Third Point; AI-for-3D-design focus) and **Polycam** ($22–24M total; capture-first) — steady niche tools; **no 2026 rounds found for either** ([pulse2](https://pulse2.com/spline-collaborative-3d-design-platform-company-secures-10-million-series-a/); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/polycam/__dAnlbEUZ1WaNOvZedtx8UUnViMs06AAa4jHziGB8ASI)). **Kaedim, Sloyd, CSM**: still operating per directory listings; no material 2026 funding/news surfaced — squeezed between Meshy/Tripo pricing and quality curves. **NVIDIA**: Edify/Omniverse persists as the ecosystem substrate (ACE, case studies) rather than a competing app. **Niantic Spatial**: no 2026 update surfaced in my searches — status unverified; flag for follow-up.
- Adjacent: **Autodesk** chose partner-not-build (World Labs $200M); **Adobe** invests via Adobe Ventures (Decart) and partner models rather than owning 3D gen.

**Category read**: Barbell. Bottom: asset generation (Meshy/Tripo/Rodin) is commoditizing fast on price ($1/model at Lowe's) but consolidating into two well-capitalized leaders. Top: "world models as product" (Marble, Genie, Oasis, Odyssey) absorbed >$3B and is converging with simulation/robotics — the exit narrative is infrastructure, not creator tools.

## Synthesis Signals

**Overcrowded / commoditizing**
- **Consumer text-to-video apps**: free Veo 3 in YouTube Shorts + CapCut bundling + Grok Imagine's $4.20/min pricing put a shrinking ceiling on undifferentiated gen apps. Sora-the-social-app's death (Mar 2026) showed even OpenAI couldn't hold attention on pure AI feeds.
- **3D asset generation**: <$1/model enterprise pricing already; two capital leaders (Meshy, Tripo) will starve the long tail (Kaedim, Sloyd, CSM quiet all year).
- **Game asset tooling**: Scenario/Layer/Rosebud are useful but thinly capitalized with weak moats vs frontier image models and general vibe-coding tools.
- **AI presentations**: Gamma won the startup race just as Microsoft and Google shipped native equivalents (Apr/Jun 2026).

**Where value is accruing**
- **Enterprise avatar video**: Synthesia ($150M+ ARR, $4B) and HeyGen ($200M ARR, break-even) — real revenue, low churn, compliance moats.
- **Licensed audio platforms**: Suno ($300M ARR, $5.4B), ElevenLabs ($11B, IPO track). The label settlements converted legal risk into a *licensing moat* that unlicensed newcomers can't replicate.
- **World models**: >$3B invested in 2026 (World Labs $1B, Decart $300M@$4B, Odyssey $310M@$1.45B) — but monetization is migrating from entertainment to simulation infrastructure (AV, robotics, e-commerce digitization), with reported strategic interest up to acquisition scale (unconfirmed Anthropic–Decart talks at $6B, Aug 2026).
- **Capital-efficient prosumer subscriptions**: Higgsfield (claimed $500M run-rate), Gamma (profitable at $100M ARR, 50 people), Mirage (revenue-linked financing) — the winning pattern is workflow + speed on top of multi-model stacks, not model ownership.

**Surprising 2026 shifts**
1. **Sora app shut down in six months** — the biggest strategic retreat by a frontier lab in consumer GenMedia; deepfake moderation was a proximate cause.
2. **Music flipped from lawsuits to licensing in ~9 months**; Udio was effectively absorbed by the labels while Suno settled from strength and bought Songkick.
3. **World-model startups pivoted from games/entertainment to driving/robotics simulation** (Decart Oasis 3, Odyssey) — the "playable worlds" consumer story is now mostly Google's (Project Genie, Jan 2026).
4. **xAI's Grok Imagine took the #1 video-model leaderboard spot** at a fraction of competitors' price — undercutting the assumption that OpenAI/Google own consumer video.
5. **Chinese apps globalized successfully** (PixVerse: 150M registered users, $439M Series C, first real-time video model) despite the US-China model race.
6. **HeyGen matched Synthesia's ARR on ~$74M of total capital** — evidence that in application-layer GenMedia, distribution + iteration speed beats capital.
