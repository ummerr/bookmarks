## PART A — Creative Agents & Workflow Layer

## Synthesis signals

**Is the workflow/agent layer becoming the primary control point? — Evidence says yes, with a twist.**
- Every layer of the stack converged on the same product in 2026: the **creative agent** (Adobe Firefly Assistant, fal Agent, Flora FAUNA, Krea Node Agent, Creatify Agent, Amazon Creative Agent, Higgsfield's consistency stack). When infra companies (fal), model companies (Runway), incumbents (Adobe), and ad platforms (Amazon) all build the same thing, that's where everyone believes the margin and lock-in live.
- The moat is shifting from model access (commodity — everyone has the same shelf) to **state**: persistent characters (Soul ID), brand constraints (Creatify's locked facts), project memory (fal Agent), reusable workflows (Figma Community, OpenArt Recipes). State = switching costs = control point.
- Exits price this in: Weavy ($4M raised → >$200M to Figma), Visual Electric → Perplexity, Leonardo → Canva. Meanwhile Sora — best model, no workflow, no B2B motion — is the layer's biggest corpse. Distribution + workflow beat raw model quality in 2026.
- The twist: the layer is being claimed **simultaneously from above (Adobe, Google Flow, Canva, Figma) and below (fal, ComfyUI)**. Independent workflow startups (Flora, Krea, Higgsfield, OpenArt) must convert 2026 growth into enterprise state/data moats before the squeeze; Higgsfield's revenue curve says at least one of them is doing it.

**Is orchestration durable, or absorbed? — Split verdict by sub-layer.**
- **Media-native inference aggregation is durable and compounding**: fal $200M→$400M annualized in ~5 months, ~$4.5B→$8B valuation path; Runware/WaveSpeed growing behind it. Drivers that don't reverse: model proliferation (majority-Chinese video supply Western apps won't integrate one-by-one), bursty GPU economics, weekly model churn making single-vendor bets irrational.
- **But pure marketplace aggregation without media-native depth gets absorbed**: Replicate → Cloudflare is the proof. And gateways (Vercel) + hyperscalers commoditize the thin "unified API" part from the sides.
- **Pricing power sits at the two ends, not the middle of the middle**: frontier model owners (Veo, Kling) set wholesale prices; consumer aggregators with consistency/workflow moats (Higgsfield ~50–80%+ implied gross margins on credit resale) set retail. Naked resellers in between compete on latency/price. fal's answer — move up into agents (Aug 2026) — is the tell: even the winning orchestrator doesn't believe raw orchestration holds margin forever.
- Net: expect a barbell. One or two media-native orchestrators (fal, plus a Cloudflare/hyperscaler-absorbed tier) survive as durable infrastructure; the workflow/agent layer above them is the strategic prize, and the companies holding **creative state + distribution** (Adobe, Figma, Canva, Google; among startups Higgsfield, Freepik/Magnific, Flora) are best positioned to own it.
